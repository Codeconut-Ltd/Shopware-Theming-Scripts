# Disclaimer

While all code has been battle-tested in large-scale
international e-commerce projects, it is not presented for production use.

As the creator of this architecture, my opinion and system are not meant
to demean the existing Shopware system in any way. The potential issues mentioned
are caused by inconsistent approaches and disorganization, not the system.

It is simply a different approach that might or might not be suitable for your project.
There is never a single "best" architecture, and you might come up with alternative or even better suited solutions.

The stack shown here proved to work well with Shopware 6+.
Future versions and advancements of technology and automation
might lead to fully different preferences.

---

_This project is crafted from years of experience working in teams and large-scale applications, as well as in complex Shopware systems that revealed the pain points and inefficiencies mentioned above._
